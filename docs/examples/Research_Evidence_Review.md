# Research Evidence Review Example

Use this pattern to separate source material, observations, interpretations, and unresolved questions.

## Review question

What evidence supports the proposed claim, and what remains uncertain?

## Source register

| ID | Source | Location | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Primary paper | `02_Research/topic/source-1.pdf` | Read |
| S2 | Dataset notes | `02_Research/topic/dataset-notes.md` | Partial |
| S3 | External commentary | `02_Research/topic/commentary.md` | Context only |

## Supported observations

- S1 reports the stated method and sample.
- S2 records missing fields that affect reproducibility.
- S3 offers an interpretation but is not primary evidence.

## Interpretations

- The result may generalize beyond the reported sample.
- The missing fields may explain the observed variance.

These are interpretations, not established facts.

## Conflicts and gaps

- S1 and S2 use different definitions for one measured variable.
- No independent replication is currently recorded.
- The dataset version used by S1 is not yet confirmed.

## Next permitted action

Confirm the dataset version and add an independent source before strengthening the claim. Do not convert an unresolved interpretation into an authoritative conclusion.

## AI boundary

An AI may organize and compare the registered sources. It must identify which statements are source-supported, inferred, disputed, or missing.
