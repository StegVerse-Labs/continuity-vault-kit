# 🤝 Presence‑Based Memory Sharing

KnowledgeVault is designed to preserve your personal memories — but life is shared with others.  
Presence‑Based Memory Sharing helps you invite the people who were **actually there** to contribute photos, stories, and perspectives, while keeping your private reflections private.

This system is **consent‑based**, **privacy‑first**, and built using Apple’s native sharing tools.

---

## 🧠 The Idea

When you create an event note (trip, holiday, gathering, milestone), you can list:

```
## People Present
- Mom
- Dad
- Alex
- Jordan

Sharing Status:
[ ] Ask to share
[ ] Private only
```

That’s all you do.

You don’t manage folders or permissions manually — the system can help you coordinate shared memories.

---

## 🔄 How Sharing Works

### 1️⃣ You record the event privately
Your event note lives in your KnowledgeVault with:
- Your thoughts
- Your emotional reflections
- Your personal context

This remains **private**.

### 2️⃣ You choose “Ask to share”
When you indicate sharing is allowed, your AI organizer (or future tool) can help you create a shared memory space.

### 3️⃣ The system helps set up shared spaces

Behind the scenes, Apple tools are used:

| Memory Type | Apple Tool | Purpose |
|-------------|------------|---------|
Shared photos & videos | iCloud Shared Album | Everyone adds media |
Shared stories & comments | Shared Apple Note | Everyone adds memories |
Optional shared documents | Shared iCloud Folder | Event plans, exports |

You stay in control and approve before anything is created.

---

## ✉️ Inviting Others

Instead of automatic sharing, the system helps you send a simple invitation like:

> “I’m organizing memories from our Colorado trip. Would you like to add your photos or stories?”

Participants can choose to:
- Join the shared album
- Add their own media
- Contribute memories
- Or ignore the invite

No one is added without consent.

---

## 🔗 How This Connects Back to Your Vault

Your private event note includes references like:

```
Shared Memory Space: Colorado Trip 2026
Participants: Mom, Dad, Alex
Shared Album: [link]
Shared Note: [link]
```

Your KnowledgeVault remains the **master timeline**.  
Shared spaces are collaborative layers that enrich the memory.

---

## 🔐 Privacy Principles

- Your vault stays private unless you explicitly share
- Emotional reflections remain in your personal notes
- Shared spaces contain only what you choose to include
- Participation is always voluntary

---

## 🌟 Why This Matters

Memories aren’t just files — they are shared human experiences.

Presence‑Based Sharing allows:
✔ Group memory building  
✔ Multiple perspectives  
✔ Collaborative storytelling  
✔ Long‑term preservation of shared moments  

All while keeping your personal archive under your control.

---

## ✨ Simple Rule

Your KnowledgeVault is the **master memory archive**.  
Shared albums, notes, and folders are **windows you open when you choose**.
