# 🛡 KnowledgeVault Backup Guide

Your KnowledgeVault is designed to preserve your life records for decades.  
Backups protect you from device loss, account issues, accidental deletion, or service shutdowns.

This guide shows a simple, reliable backup method using the **3‑2‑1 rule**.

---

## 📦 The 3‑2‑1 Backup Rule

You should have:

**3 copies of your vault**  
**2 different storage types**  
**1 offline copy**

This is easy to maintain from an iPhone or iPad.

---

## 🟢 Copy #1 — Your Live Working Vault

**Location:** iCloud Drive → KnowledgeVault

This is your active vault where you add notes, records, and media.

✔ Automatically syncs across Apple devices  
⚠️ Still tied to your Apple account

---

## 🟡 Copy #2 — Secondary Cloud Backup

Choose at least one:

- Google Drive  
- Dropbox  

### How to create it

Once per month:

1. Open the **Files** app  
2. Find your **KnowledgeVault** folder  
3. Long‑press it → Tap **Copy**  
4. Go to Google Drive or Dropbox  
5. Paste into a folder called:

```
Backups/KnowledgeVault
```

This protects you if you ever lose access to your Apple account.

---

## 🔵 Copy #3 — Offline Backup (Most Important)

This protects you from cloud shutdowns, account lockouts, or ransomware.

### Step 1 — Create a ZIP archive

1. In **Files**, long‑press **KnowledgeVault**  
2. Tap **Compress**  
3. Rename the file to something like:

```
KnowledgeVault_Backup_2026‑Q1.zip
```

### Step 2 — Store it offline

Move this ZIP file to:

- An external SSD or USB drive  
- An encrypted drive  
- A trusted family member’s drive  
- A safe deposit box (for long‑term archival)

Do this **every 3 months**.

---

## 🔁 Optional: Keep Versioned Snapshots

Inside your vault, you may keep older snapshots in:

```
_Archive/Snapshots/
```

This lets you see what your vault looked like at different times in your life.

---

## 🔐 Sensitive Data Tip

If you store highly sensitive records:

1. Put them in a subfolder like:
   `03_Records/_Private/`
2. Zip and encrypt that folder separately
3. Store it offline in a secure location

---

## 📅 Recommended Schedule

| Task | How Often | Time Required |
|------|-----------|---------------|
Cloud copy to Google/Dropbox | Monthly | ~2 minutes |
Offline ZIP backup | Quarterly | ~5 minutes |
Check backups open properly | Once per year | ~10 minutes |

Set calendar reminders — it makes this effortless.

---

## 🧠 Why This Matters

Your KnowledgeVault is a long‑term memory system.  
Backups ensure your memories survive:

- Device loss  
- Account lockout  
- Cloud shutdown  
- Accidental deletion  
- Service changes over time

With this backup plan, your vault becomes truly resilient.
