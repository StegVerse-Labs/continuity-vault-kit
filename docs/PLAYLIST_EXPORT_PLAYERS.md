# 🎵 Playlist Export — Compatible Music Players

KnowledgeVault can export playlists in **open formats** so your memories can be turned into music playlists in many different apps.

When you use **Export Playlist**, you may see options like:

- M3U8
- XSPF
- Text list
- CSV list

Here’s what those work with.

---

## 📁 M3U / M3U8 Playlists

This is the most widely supported playlist format for **local music players**.

Common apps that accept M3U/M3U8:

- VLC Media Player (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows)
- Kodi Media Center
- Plex (for locally hosted music libraries)
- MediaMonkey
- JRiver Media Center
- Many desktop music players

⚠️ Note:  
These players usually expect **music files you own locally**, not streaming-only tracks.

---

## 📄 XSPF Playlists

XSPF is an open playlist format used by some advanced players.

Often supported by:

- VLC (desktop)
- Kodi
- Some desktop media managers

This format is mostly for archival and advanced use.

---

## 📝 Text / CSV Song Lists

These are simple lists of:

Artist – Song Title

These files are useful because:

- They are easy to read
- They can be imported into tools later
- AI can use them to recreate playlists in streaming apps
- They never become obsolete

---

## 🎧 Streaming Services

Streaming services don’t directly import M3U or XSPF files.

### Apple Music
Best experience for iPhone users.

A Shortcut can:
- Read your song list
- Find matches in Apple Music
- Create a playlist automatically

### Spotify / YouTube Music
These may require:

- Manual playlist building
- Third-party import tools
- A future KnowledgeVault “Playlist Bridge” service

Even if direct import isn’t available, your song memories remain safely stored and reusable later.

---

## 💡 Why This Matters

You are not locking your memories into one music service.

Your playlist memories exist as:

- Structured song logs
- Open playlist files
- Human-readable lists

This means future tools — or future AI — can rebuild your playlists anytime.
