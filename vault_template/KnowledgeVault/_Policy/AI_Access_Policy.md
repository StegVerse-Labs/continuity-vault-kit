# AI Access Policy

Default stance: least privilege.

## Safe-by-default sharing
- Share `_Index/` first
- Share specific topic folders only as needed
- Avoid placing secrets directly into notes unless encrypted

## Sensitive content handling
- Keep highly sensitive records encrypted (recommended: Cryptomator-compatible folder)
- If sharing with AI, create a redacted summary Markdown instead of full records

## Audit practice
When you share documents with AI, record:
- what was shared
- when
- purpose
in a note under `01_Notes/` (optional)
