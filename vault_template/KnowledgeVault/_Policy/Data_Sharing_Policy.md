## What "sharing" means

If you opt in, your selected data may be:

1. **Indexed** — structured for search and matching with other users' data
2. **Aggregated** — combined with matching data from other users (never sold individually)
3. **Used** — by researchers, advertisers, content platforms, or other entities
4. **Compensated** — if revenue is generated from aggregated use, you may receive a share

You do not share directly with buyers. StegVerse handles aggregation and anonymization.

## How to opt out

1. Uncheck the boxes above
2. Add an opt-out record to the consent table
3. Future sharing stops immediately
4. Already-aggregated data may remain in existing datasets (per the StegVerse data retention policy)

---

## Revenue sharing (if applicable)

If your shared data generates revenue:

- Revenue is calculated at the **aggregate dataset** level, not per-user
- Your share is proportional to your contribution to the dataset
- Payouts are periodic and require a valid payout method (set up separately)
- You can view estimated earnings through StegVerse tools (if connected)

**This is not guaranteed income.** It is a potential benefit of opt-in sharing, not the purpose of your vault.
