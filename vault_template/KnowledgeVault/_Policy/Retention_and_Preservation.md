# Retention & Preservation

Preferred formats:
- Notes: `.md`
- Documents: `.pdf` (PDF/A when possible)
- Data: `.csv`, `.json`
- Images: `.jpg`, `.png`

Quarterly:
- Create an offline encrypted backup of the entire vault

Yearly:
- Create a “year snapshot” export for long-term cold storage
