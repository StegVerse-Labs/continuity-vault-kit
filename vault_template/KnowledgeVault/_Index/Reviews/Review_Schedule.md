# Review Schedule

These are suggested review intervals. They are flexible.

---

## 🚀 Active Projects
Review every **1–3 months**

Purpose:
- Capture progress
- Adjust goals
- Avoid losing track of long efforts

---

## 👥 Close Relationships
Review every **6–12 months**

Purpose:
- Note life changes
- Update connection context
- Preserve evolving relationships

---

## 📍 Important Places
Review every **1–2 years**

Purpose:
- Record moves, changes, or shifting significance

---

## 🏢 Organizations
Review when involvement changes

Purpose:
- Capture role transitions
- Preserve career or community phases

---

## 🧠 Life Timeline
Review yearly

Purpose:
- Add summary of major life themes
- Ensure continuity across years
