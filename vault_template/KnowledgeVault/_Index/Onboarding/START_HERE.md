# Welcome to Your KnowledgeVault

You don’t need to organize your whole life today.

This quick setup helps create the basic structure so future memories have context.

Take your time. Rough answers are perfect.

---

## Step 1 — Life Chapters

Open:
👉 `Life_Chapters.md`

Write short descriptions of major phases of your life.
Just a few sentences per chapter.

---

## Step 2 — Important People

Open:
👉 `_Entities/People/_Backfill_Key_People.md`

List the people who matter most in your life story.
You can create detailed profiles later.

---

## Step 3 — Important Places

Open:
👉 `_Entities/Places/_Backfill_Key_Places.md`

List homes, cities, or places that shaped your life.

---

## Step 4 — Organizations

Open:
👉 `_Entities/Organizations/_Backfill_Orgs.md`

Add schools, employers, communities, and teams.

---

## Step 5 — Ongoing Life Projects

Open:
👉 `_Entities/Projects/_Backfill_Projects.md`

List things that are still unfolding in your life.

---

That’s it.

From here, just start logging memories when they happen.
The system will slowly build around you.
