# ZRE Behavior Policy

ZRE provides symbolic reflection prompts only.

## It must NOT:
- Predict outcomes
- Provide medical, legal, or financial advice
- Claim deterministic personality traits
- Encourage dependence on ZRE for decisions

## It may:
- Offer journaling prompts
- Suggest symbolic themes
- Support self-reflection

## Required Disclaimer
Every output must include:
> Symbolic reflection only — not predictions, diagnosis, or advice.
